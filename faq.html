Orders & payment

How do I place an order?

Browse our catalog, add products to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately and a shipping confirmation with tracking within 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), PayPal, and — for verified healthcare organizations — invoicing on net-30 terms.

Can I get a quote before ordering?

Absolutely. Use the "Request a quote" button on any product page or contact us directly. We respond to quote requests within 24 hours.

Shipping & delivery

How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is a flat $14.99 within the continental US. Orders over $150 ship free. Express and international options are calculated at checkout.

How fast will I receive my order?

In-stock items dispatch the same business day if ordered before 3:00 PM local time. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days. Express options are available.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to over 45 countries. International shipping costs and duties are calculated at checkout. Some products may have regional restrictions based on local regulations.

Returns & warranty

What is your return policy?

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. For hygiene reasons, opened PPE, wound care, and sterile products cannot be returned.

Are products covered by warranty?

Most diagnostic and durable equipment carries a manufacturer warranty — typically 12–24 months. Warranty details are included with each product. Contact us for warranty claims and we'll handle the process with the manufacturer.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damaged item and packaging. We'll arrange a replacement or refund immediately — no questions asked.

Products & certifications

Are your products certified?

Yes. All products carry the certifications required for their category — CE marking for the EU, FDA registration for the US, and ISO 13485 quality management. Product-specific certificates are available on request.

Do you provide product documentation?

Yes. Instruction manuals, spec sheets, and certificates of conformity are available for every product. Request them on the product page or via our contact form.

Can you source products not listed on your site?

Often yes. We work with a global network of manufacturers. Send us a description or part number and we'll try to source it for you.

Bulk & wholesale

Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes. Orders above certain thresholds qualify for automatic discounts, and larger contracts (hospitals, tenders, government) get customized pricing. Contact us for a tailored quote.

Do you support purchase orders (POs)?

Yes — for verified healthcare organizations we accept POs and offer net-30 terms. Contact our sales team to set up an account.

Account & support

How do I contact support?

Email support@medsupply.com, call +1 (800) 555-0199, or use our contact form. We respond within one business day.

Do I need an account to order?

No — you can check out as a guest. However, creating an account lets you save addresses, track orders, and access order history.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help with anything — from product specs to bulk pricing.
