About MedSupply

Medical supplies, delivered with care since 2007.

We equip thousands of clinics, hospitals, and home-care providers with certified medical equipment — reliably, ethically, and fast.

Our story

Built by clinicians, for clinicians

MedSupply started with a simple frustration: ordering medical supplies was slow, opaque, and unreliable. Our founders — a nurse and a biomedical engineer — decided to fix it.

Today we serve over 12,000 healthcare organizations across 45 countries, carrying products from the world's most trusted manufacturers. Every item we ship is verified, certified, and backed by real human support.

We don't just sell boxes. We help clinicians stay focused on what matters: their patients.
